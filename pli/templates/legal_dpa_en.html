Data processing agreement (organizers)

Draft for legal review — not yet reviewed by counsel. Version française : français.

This agreement under Art. 28 GDPR applies between the event organizer (controller) and {{ company_name }}, {{ company_address }}, SIREN {{ company_siren }} (processor) for every event the organizer runs on the platform. It is accepted at event creation.

  1. Subject and duration. Operation of a mutual-declaration round for the organizer's event, from round creation until reveal or void, at which point all participant data is erased by design.
  2. Nature and purpose. Collection of participant email addresses (stored as keyed hashes and encrypted blobs) and sealed declarations; computation of reciprocal pairs; notification of reciprocal pairs only.
  3. Categories of data subjects and data. Event participants; email addresses and declaration graphs, the latter being data liable to reveal aspects of affective life (Art. 9 GDPR), processed with the participant's explicit consent.
  4. Instructions. The processor acts only on the documented instructions embodied in the platform's published mechanics. The organizer acknowledges that the mechanics themselves forbid certain instructions: the processor will not disclose rosters, declarations, or match data to the organizer, because non-disclosure is the service.
  5. Confidentiality & security. Keyed hashing and authenticated encryption at rest, per-round keys destroyed at reveal, no request-level logs, no backups of participation data, deletion with storage compaction at reveal. Personnel authorised to operate the platform are bound to confidentiality.
  6. Sub-processors. Hosting and transactional email (list published in the repository's GDPR documentation). The organizer is informed of changes in advance and may object by cancelling the event, which deletes everything.
  7. Data subject rights. Given the design, most requests are satisfied by the reveal-time erasure itself; the processor assists the organizer with any request received during a live round.
  8. Breach notification. The processor notifies the organizer without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting the event.
  9. Erasure. Automatic and unconditional at reveal or void; no copies survive. An audit of this property is available to the organizer in the form of the public source code and its test suite.
