Privacy policy

Draft for legal review — not yet reviewed by counsel. French version: français.

Controller / processor. The platform is operated by {{ company_name }}, {{ company_address }}, SIREN {{ company_siren }} ("we"). For the default community we act as data controller. For events created by an organizer, the organizer is the data controller and we act as processor under the data processing agreement. Contact: {{ company_contact }}.

What this service is. A mutual-declaration escrow. You privately name up to three people; if — and only if — a named person names you back, each of you receives the other's email address at the published reveal time. Nothing else is ever disclosed, to anyone.

Data we process, and for how long.

  1. Your email address, if you join a round: stored as a keyed hash (HMAC-SHA256) plus an encrypted blob (AES-256-GCM under a per-round key). Deleted at the reveal, at the latest. Legal basis: consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR); the declaration content can reveal information about your affective life, which we treat under Art. 9(2)(a) — explicit consent given by the act of declaring, after this notice.
  2. Your declarations: stored only as pairs of keyed hashes. Never shown to anyone — including you — and deleted at the reveal, matched or not. The database is then compacted so deleted data is not recoverable from the file.
  3. Organizer accounts: the organizer's email address, plan, and configuration, kept for the life of the account. Legal basis: contract (Art. 6(1)(b)).
  4. Abuse reports: reason, optional free text, and a keyed hash of the reporter's IP (to deduplicate). No reporter identity is stored. Legal basis: legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f), platform safety).
  5. Suppression list: a keyed hash of addresses whose mail bounced or who complained, kept so we never mail them again. Legal basis: legitimate interest and Art. 21 objection handling.

What we never do. No profiles. No advertising. No sale or sharing of data. No analytics on participants. No request-level access logs. No backups of the participation database — deletion means deletion. Mail is sent through a transactional provider configured without message archiving.

The honest limit. While a round is live, the operator technically holds key material that could reconstruct who named whom. That window closes at the reveal, when the data is destroyed. We state this because it is true; do not participate if this trust is more than you wish to extend.

Your rights. Access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection (Arts. 15–21 GDPR): write to {{ company_contact }}. Note that after a reveal there is nothing left to access or erase, and during a round we cannot show you your own declarations (an anti-coercion property). You may lodge a complaint with the CNIL (cnil.fr).

Age. The service is not directed at children. You must be at least 15 (the French age of digital consent, Art. 45 of the Loi Informatique et Libertés) — organizers of a given event may set a higher bar.
